# Product Marketing Context

*Last updated: 2026-03-31*

## Product Overview
**One-liner:** All-in-one membership and event management platform that replaces the WhatsApp groups, spreadsheets, Eventbrite, and PayPal that community clubs are stitched together on.
**What it does:** GatherGrove consolidates member management, dues collection, event coordination, communications, and mobile apps into one platform purpose-built for community clubs and small nonprofits. It eliminates the 5+ disconnected tools club admins juggle daily.
**Product category:** Club management software / membership management software
**Product type:** SaaS (web app + native mobile apps)
**Business model:** Flat monthly/annual subscription. Three tiers: Seed ($9/mo, ≤100 members), Grow ($29/mo, ≤200 members), Unlimited ($200/mo). All plans include a 30-day free trial (credit card required). Zero platform fees on payments — only standard Stripe processing rates apply.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Community clubs, hobby groups, youth sports leagues, adult recreational sports organizations, volunteer-run nonprofits, professional associations — typically 10-500 members, run by volunteer admins.
**Decision-makers:** Club presidents, league commissioners, board members, volunteer coordinators, team managers, treasurers — almost always unpaid volunteers.
**Primary use case:** Replace the patchwork of spreadsheets, WhatsApp, PayPal/Venmo, Google Forms, and Eventbrite with one integrated platform.
**Jobs to be done:**
- Collect dues and event fees without chasing people down
- Coordinate events, registrations, and attendance tracking
- Communicate with members without managing 5 different channels
**Use cases:**
- Youth sports registration and fee collection across multiple age groups
- Adult rec league scheduling, payments, and attendance
- Book club or hobby group member coordination and event planning
- Nonprofit volunteer scheduling, hour tracking, and communications

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Volunteer club admins spend hours on administrative tasks that should take minutes because their operations are spread across 5+ disconnected tools.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Eventbrite is events-only and charges per-ticket fees
- TeamSnap is sports-only and expensive ($9.99-24.99/mo per team)
- Wild Apricot is too complex and expensive ($60+/month) for small clubs
- Spreadsheets + WhatsApp cost nothing but create massive coordination overhead
**What it costs them:** Hours of volunteer time per week on admin, lost revenue from uncollected dues, member churn from poor communication, scattered data with no reporting.
**Emotional tension:** Burnout. Volunteer admins didn't sign up to be full-time data entry clerks. They want to run their club, not manage spreadsheets.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** TeamSnap — sports-focused, expensive per-team pricing, no general club features
**Direct:** Wild Apricot — full-featured but complex, expensive ($60+/mo), built for associations not clubs
**Secondary:** Eventbrite — events only, charges attendee fees, no membership management
**Secondary:** Spond — free but limited, no payment collection, no member portal
**Indirect:** Spreadsheets + WhatsApp + Venmo — zero cost but massive admin burden, the status quo we replace

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- Zero platform fees on payments (clubs keep 100% of collected fees)
- Lowest entry price in market ($9/mo vs. $60+ for Wild Apricot, $10-25/team for TeamSnap)
- Complete platform (events + payments + communications + members + mobile) in one tool
- 30-day free trial on all plans
**How we do it differently:** Flat subscription instead of per-transaction or per-team pricing. One integrated platform instead of point solutions.
**Why that's better:** Treasurers trust that every dollar collected goes to the club. Admins manage everything from one dashboard instead of five tabs.
**Why customers choose us:** Price (cheapest full-featured option), simplicity (replaces 5 tools), and zero platform fees (important for budget-conscious volunteer orgs).

## Objections
| Objection | Response |
|-----------|----------|
| "We already use spreadsheets and they're free" | Free in dollars, expensive in time. How many hours per week do you spend on admin? GatherGrove automates dues collection, event registration, and communications — that time goes back to actually running your club. |
| "We tried software before and it was too complicated" | GatherGrove is built for volunteer admins, not IT departments. Most clubs are up and running in under 30 minutes. And if you get stuck, our support team helps you set up. |
| "We can't justify the cost to our board" | At $9/month, GatherGrove costs less than one pizza party. It pays for itself the first month by automating dues collection alone — most clubs recover 15-30% more in unpaid dues. |

**Anti-persona:** Large professional associations (500+ staff-managed members) who need enterprise features, SSO, or custom integrations. Enterprise orgs needing SOC 2 compliance. Clubs that are truly happy with their current setup and have no admin pain.

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** Frustration with chasing payments, scattered member data, WhatsApp chaos, season-start registration scramble
**Pull:** One dashboard, automated dues reminders, mobile app members actually use, professional-looking event pages
**Habit:** "We've always done it this way," familiar tools (even if painful), sunk cost in existing spreadsheets
**Anxiety:** "Will my members actually use it?", "What if I lose our data?", migration effort, learning curve
